Who we are

Independent telecommunications infrastructure provider.

Del Connect owns, operates, manages and develops shared telecommunications infrastructure for mobile network operators and ICT providers.

Del Connect is evolving from a traditional telecommunications infrastructure provider into a full InfraCo — owning, developing, acquiring and managing tower assets and related solutions across the African continent, with a deliberate focus on the peri-urban areas that are often underserved by conventional infrastructure investment.

A division of New Generation Group, alongside Circle Tel SA, Delphius Commercial and Industrial Technologies, New ExGen and Account Caddie.
