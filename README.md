## 1. What Del Connect Is

Del Connect is New Generation Group's **tower leasing and infrastructure co-location** division. It supports the Group's transition from a traditional TowerCo (tower ownership/leasing) model to a full **InfraCo** model — i.e. not just leasing tower space, but managing shared network infrastructure end-to-end for the operators who depend on it.

- **Tagline:** Tower Leasing & InfraCo
- **Core services (generic, pending final copy):**
  - Tower leasing to network operators (MNOs, ISPs, WISPs)
  - Co-location of radio, transmission, and network equipment on shared infrastructure
  - Site management and access for installation/maintenance crews
  - Supporting network densification (4G/5G rollout, fibre backhaul, rural connectivity)
- **Who it serves:** network operators and telecoms infrastructure players who need physical site access without owning/building their own towers
- **Status:** Early-stage / "coming soon" on the Group's public site — the division is being stood up, so exact service scope and commercial model are still being defined.

**Why this model matters:** shared infrastructure lets operators avoid duplicating costly tower builds, speeds up rollout, and reduces the environmental/logistical footprint of network expansion — directly reinforcing NGG's stated commitment to sustainable, efficient digital infrastructure.

## 2. How Del Connect Aligns to New Generation Group

### Group purpose
> "To bridge humanity and technology by delivering purpose-fit solutions that enable businesses, institutions, and communities to operate smarter, grow sustainably, and thrive in a rapidly evolving digital world."

Del Connect operationalises this at the most literal, physical layer: **the towers and shared sites that make connectivity possible in the first place.** Where other divisions build platforms, systems, or services on top of connectivity, Del Connect provides part of the underlying infrastructure layer those services run on.

### Where it sits among the divisions
New Generation Group's family of divisions (per the Group's live site): **New ExGen, Circle Tel, Delphius, Del Connect, SHERQ 365, Account Caddie.**

- **Circle Tel** (IT & connectivity solutions) and other Group telecoms/digital services stand to directly benefit from Del Connect's infrastructure — an internal synergy: Del Connect can host or enable the physical layer that Circle Tel's connectivity offerings need.
- **Delphius** (engineering-led, technical precision) and Del Connect share a technical, infrastructure-first character — both lean on "engineering discipline" as a brand trait, just applied to different layers (systems vs. physical sites).
- **SHERQ 365** (safety, health, environment, risk, quality/compliance) is highly relevant to Del Connect operationally — tower and site work is safety-critical, so SHERQ 365's compliance discipline likely underpins how Del Connect runs site access and maintenance.

*(Note: the Group's brand-story copy in the CI Guide references a slightly different division list — NGMC, Intelliview, Delphius, Circle Tel, Del Connect, Account Caddie — than the current live site's list above. Worth flagging to NGG as a copy inconsistency to reconcile, not something for us to resolve unilaterally.)*

### Brand archetype fit
The Group's overall archetype is **The Inventor/Creator** (curious, visionary, grounded). Each division carries a complementary sub-archetype:

- **Del Connect = The Builder + The Guide** — reliable, practical, solution-focused, and positioned as a trusted partner that brings clarity, insight, and direction.
- This is a deliberately *steadier, more grounded* archetype than the Group's Inventor energy — fitting for a division whose product is physical, dependable infrastructure rather than novel invention. It still ladders up to the Group promise of reliable, sustainable, cost-effective solutions.

### Tone of voice fit
Del Connect: **clear, concise, professional, human and collaborative** — grounded in precision and outcomes, clarity without unnecessary complexity. This is consistent with the Group's overall voice principle ("thoughtful, not hype-driven; confident, not arrogant; clear, not overly technical") — Del Connect's version simply skews slightly warmer/more partnership-oriented, matching its role as a partner to operators rather than an end-customer-facing product.

## 3. Brand Identity (from the NGG Group CI Guide)

*Source: New Generation Group Brand Identity Guidelines, v. 05/03/2025 — Del Connect sections (pp. 13, 15, 20, 29).*

### Logo
- Icon (signal/tower mark) + wordmark, "DEL" in orange, "CONNECT" in grey.
- Must always be used as provided — no stretching, distortion, or unauthorised modification.
- Clear space and proportions are defined in the guide (icon : gap : wordmark ratio, minimum sizing).

### Typography
- **Montserrat** — primary typeface, used in the logo and dominant brand communication.
- **Arial** — secondary, for formal documents (Office/Google Docs, PDFs).
- **Poppins** — complementary/dynamic pairing font for marketing collateral.

### Brand feel
> "Del Connect presents a clean, structured, and modern feel rooted in reliability and precision. The visual style is calm and confident, balancing technical clarity with a sense of partnership and dependable connectivity."

### Imagery style
Telecoms towers, signal/network graphics, technicians and engineers on-site, server/rack cabling, global connectivity visuals, and professional partnership shots (handshakes, meetings). Imagery should feel technical and infrastructure-grounded, not abstract/corporate stock.

### Stationery mockups (reference)
Letterhead, business card, and envelope mockups already exist (matching CI Guide p. 29) — white stock, orange accent bar/edge, dark grey/black secondary card back with a subtle circular pattern texture, "DEL CONNECT" logo top-left. These match the screenshots shared for this project and should be treated as the confirmed visual direction for Del Connect stationery.
